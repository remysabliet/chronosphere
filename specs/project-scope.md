<h1 style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;">Scope 1: Minimum Viable Product (MVP)</h1>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;"><strong>Text based questions</strong></p>

<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;"><strong>User Authentication:</strong> Allow users to create accounts, log in, and manage their profiles.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;"><strong>Question Management:</strong> Implement functionality to create, edit, and delete text-based questions.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;"><strong>Quiz Taking:</strong> Enable users to take quizzes composed of text-based questions and receive immediate feedback on their performance.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;"><strong>Scoring System:</strong> Implement a basic scoring system to track users' quiz scores.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;"><strong>AI-driven Feedback Evaluation:</strong> Integrate AI algorithms to evaluate users' quiz performance and provide feedback on strengths and weaknesses.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;"><strong>Dynamic Quiz Generation:</strong> Develop algorithms to dynamically generate quizzes based on users' strengths and weaknesses, prioritizing questions that target weaker areas.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;"><strong>Scoring Visualization:</strong> Provide visualizations or diagrams to illustrate users' strong and weak points based on quiz performance.</p>

<h1 style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;">Scope 2: Enhanced AI-driven Adaptive Quizzing</h1>

<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;">By focusing on AI-driven feedback evaluation and adaptive quiz generation, you can create a personalized learning experience that maximizes user engagement and performance. This approach ensures that the application evolves dynamically to meet users' learning goals and preferences.</p>

<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;"><strong>Advanced Feedback Analysis:</strong> Enhance AI capabilities to perform in-depth analysis of users' quiz performance, identifying specific areas of improvement and providing targeted feedback.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;"><strong>Personalized Learning Paths:</strong> Implement AI-driven algorithms to create personalized learning paths for users, recommending quizzes and study materials based on individual strengths, weaknesses, and learning preferences.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;"><strong>Real-time Adaptation:</strong> Enable quizzes to adapt in real-time based on users' responses, dynamically adjusting question difficulty and content to optimize learning outcomes.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;"><strong>Interactive Feedback:</strong> Develop interactive feedback mechanisms that allow users to explore their strengths and weaknesses in more detail, providing insights and recommendations for improvement.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;"><strong>Integration with Learning Resources:</strong> Integrate with external educational resources or content libraries to provide additional learning materials tailored to users' needs.</p>
