<h1 style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;">Scope 1: Minimum Viable Product (MVP)</h1>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;">Text based questions</p>

<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;">User Authentication: Allow users to create accounts, log in, and manage their profiles.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;">Question Management: Implement functionality to create, edit, and delete text-based questions.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;">Quiz Taking: Enable users to take quizzes composed of text-based questions and receive immediate feedback on their performance.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;">Scoring System: Implement a basic scoring system to track users' quiz scores.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;">AI-driven Feedback Evaluation: Integrate AI algorithms to evaluate users' quiz performance and provide feedback on strengths and weaknesses.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;">Dynamic Quiz Generation: Develop algorithms to dynamically generate quizzes based on users' strengths and weaknesses, prioritizing questions that target weaker areas.</p>
<p style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;">Scoring Visualization: Provide visualizations or diagrams to illustrate users' strong and weak points based on quiz performance.</p>

<h1 style="font-family: 'Nunito', sans-serif;">Scope 2: Enhanced AI-driven Adaptive Quizzing</h1>

<p>By focusing on AI-driven feedback evaluation and adaptive quiz generation, you can create a personalized learning experience that maximizes user engagement and performance. This approach ensures that the application evolves dynamically to meet users' learning goals and preferences. </p>

Advanced Feedback Analysis: Enhance AI capabilities to perform in-depth analysis of users' quiz performance, identifying specific areas of improvement and providing targeted feedback.
Personalized Learning Paths: Implement AI-driven algorithms to create personalized learning paths for users, recommending quizzes and study materials based on individual strengths, weaknesses, and learning preferences.
Real-time Adaptation: Enable quizzes to adapt in real-time based on users' responses, dynamically adjusting question difficulty and content to optimize learning outcomes.
Interactive Feedback: Develop interactive feedback mechanisms that allow users to explore their strengths and weaknesses in more detail, providing insights and recommendations for improvement.
Integration with Learning Resources: Integrate with external educational resources or content libraries to provide additional learning materials tailored to users' needs.
